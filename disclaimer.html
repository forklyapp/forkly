Nutrition & Medical Disclaimer

Effective date: June 11, 2026 · Pantreo LLC, Clearfield, Utah
The short version: Pantreo is a meal planning and food tracking tool, not a doctor or dietitian. Its AI estimates can be wrong. Always verify anything that matters to your health — especially allergies.

Not Medical or Nutritional Advice

Nothing in Pantreo — including AI-generated meal plans, recipes, calorie targets, TDEE/BMR calculations, macro estimates, photo-based food analysis, and cutting/bulking guidance — constitutes medical, dietary, or health advice. Pantreo does not create a doctor–patient or dietitian–client relationship. Content is provided for general informational and organizational purposes only.

AI Estimates Are Approximations

Pantreo's nutritional values are generated by artificial intelligence and are estimates that can be wrong. Photo scanning cannot precisely determine portion sizes, hidden ingredients (oils, butter, sugar, sauces), or preparation methods. Text-based estimates depend on how a food is described. Treat every number as a rough starting point, not a measurement.

Allergies and Dietary Restrictions

While you can tell Pantreo about dietary restrictions, AI-generated recipes and meal plans may still include or fail to flag ingredients you need to avoid. If you have a food allergy, intolerance, celiac disease, or a medically required diet, you must independently check every ingredient in every recipe before cooking or eating. Do not rely on Pantreo for allergen safety.

Talk to a Professional

Consult a qualified healthcare provider or registered dietitian before making significant changes to your diet or starting a calorie-restricted, weight-loss, or weight-gain program — especially if you have a medical condition, take medication, are pregnant or nursing, are recovering from disordered eating, or are under a clinician's care. Stop and seek professional guidance if a dietary change makes you feel unwell.

Your Responsibility

Your use of Pantreo's calorie and macro guidance is at your own discretion and risk. Individual nutritional needs vary widely; what the AI suggests for a general goal may not be appropriate for you. Pantreo LLC is not responsible for outcomes resulting from reliance on generated content.

Emergencies

Pantreo is not for emergencies. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 (or your local emergency number) immediately.
