Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pantreo LLC (“Pantreo,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information about you when you use the Pantreo website, applications, and services (collectively, the “Service”). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

Pantreo LLC is a Utah limited liability company based in Clearfield, Utah, United States. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at pantreo.support@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information:

Information you provide directly

  • Account information — your name, email address, and password when you create an account. Passwords are stored in hashed form by our authentication provider; we never see your plain-text password.
  • Profile and preference information — dietary preferences and restrictions, foods you dislike, household size, cuisine preferences, and your nutrition goals.
  • Health and fitness information — information you choose to enter such as height, weight, activity level, calorie and macronutrient targets, and the food and water you log. You provide this voluntarily to use the tracking features.
  • Content you create — meal plans you generate, recipes you save, and any text you enter into planning or tracking tools.
  • Communications — information you provide when you contact support or otherwise communicate with us.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data — how you interact with the Service, features used, and actions taken.
  • Device and technical data — IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, and similar technical identifiers.
  • Local storage — we store certain data in your browser’s local storage (such as authentication tokens and your preferences) to keep you signed in and remember your settings. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

Payment information

Payments are processed by Stripe. When you subscribe to a paid plan, your payment card details are collected and processed directly by Stripe under its own privacy policy. We do not collect, see, or store your full payment card number. We receive limited information from Stripe such as your subscription status and the last four digits of your card.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Service, including generating meal plans and tracking your nutrition.
  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you.
  • To process subscriptions, payments, and referrals.
  • To personalize your experience based on your preferences and goals.
  • To communicate with you about your account, updates, security notices, and support requests.
  • To improve, troubleshoot, and develop new features.
  • To detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, security issues, and violations of our Terms.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Artificial intelligence processing

Pantreo uses artificial intelligence to generate meal plans, recipes, and nutritional estimates. To do this, certain information you provide — such as your dietary preferences, food descriptions, and meal requests — is transmitted to our AI provider, Anthropic, which operates the Claude AI models, and is processed on our behalf to generate responses. We do not send your name, email, or payment information to the AI provider. AI-generated output may be inaccurate and should be reviewed (see our Nutrition & Medical Disclaimer).

However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information.

  • Export a copy of your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, email pantreo.support@gmail.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

California residents
